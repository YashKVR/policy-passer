# PolicyPasser

## PolicyPasser

Policy Passer takes a policy copy and the claim papers, then checks whether submitting those papers would get the claim approved. If it would not, it names the mistakes in the papers and explains why the claim would be rejected.

### Your role in the build

The documents in `docs/` are written as PolicyPasser's own. The spec comes from PolicyPasser's head of claims operations, and each later brief from the people at PolicyPasser who asked for the work. Treat them as the customer's requirements. I am PolicyPasser's forward deployed engineer and you are my coding agent. The student directs the work and reviews your plans and builds. You plan, build and check the work as `docs/PROCESS.md` describes.

I am also here to learn. Write plans I can review: say what each phase builds and why you chose that approach. Explain your work when they ask, at the level I ask for. Then deliver the build to the standards in `docs/`.
